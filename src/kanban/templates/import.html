CSV format

One row per card. Only title is required — everything else is optional.

ColumnMeaning
titleThe card's title (required; name also works)
listWhich list it goes in — created if it doesn't exist yet (kanban boards only; column/status also work)
dueA due date — natural language works too, e.g. "tomorrow", "in 3 days"
startA start date, same format as due
tagsSpace- or comma-separated, with or without #
prioritylow, medium or high
notesThe card's notes (Markdown works; body also works)
doneyes/true/1 marks it already completed
